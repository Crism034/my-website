Find Professional Help

BetterHelp

BetterHelp will match you to one of 31,000 licensed therapists based upon your location, preferences, and therapist availability.

Visit BetterHelp
Zocdoc

Zocdoc is an online healthcare marketplace that helps patients find nearby doctors, dentists, and specialists who accept their specific insurance plans. Users can browse verified patient reviews, compare real-time availability, and instantly book in-person or telehealth appointments online.

Visit Zocdoc
Psychology Today Therapist Finder

Psychology Today offers a welcoming, easy-to-navigate directory to help you connect with licensed therapists, psychiatrists, and support groups tailored to your needs. Alongside its provider search, the site shares compassionate, expert-written articles and guides to help you better understand mental health, relationships, and emotional well-being.

Visit Psychology Today
Open Path Collective

Open Path Collective is a welcoming nonprofit network that helps individuals, couples, and families access affordable, compassionate therapy when insurance isn't an option or out-of-pocket costs feel out of reach. By connecting you with licensed mental health professionals offering reduced-rate in-person and online sessions, it makes finding the right support more accessible and stress-free.

Visit Open Path Collective

Self-Care Resources

Calm

Calm is a digital wellness app and website designed to help you gently manage everyday stress, ease anxiety, and improve your sleep through accessible audio programs. It offers a soothing, easy-to-navigate library of guided meditations, breathing exercises, and relaxing sleep stories to help you bring a moment of peace into your daily routine.

Visit Calm
Headspace

Headspace is a mindfulness and meditation platform designed to make building healthy mental habits feel approachable and easy for everyday life. It offers a friendly, guided library of meditations, sleep exercises, and focus tools to help you gently manage stress, reduce anxiety, and find a little more balance.

Visit Headspace
Insight Timer

Insight Timer is a gentle, community-centered meditation platform that offers an incredibly large library of completely free guided meditations, soothing music, and wellness talks. It is designed to help you ease anxiety, improve your sleep, and find moments of calm without the barrier of a costly subscription.

Visit Insight Timer
