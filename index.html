How to Support a Friend Who Is Struggling

Peer Guide
1. Lead with Presence, Not Pressure

You don't need all the answers. Try saying: "I care about you and I'm right here. We don't have to fix everything tonight—do you want me to just listen, or should we text a helpline together?"

2. Reach Out Side-by-Side

Tap any Copy button above to paste a helpline straight into a message to your friend, or sit with them while they text 741741 or dial 988.

Privacy, Safety & What to Expect

FAQ
Are these hotlines really free and anonymous?

Yes. Every resource listed on HelpHub Aurora is 100% free, and you never have to share your real name, address, or insurance information.

How does the Quick Exit button protect my privacy?

Clicking Quick Exit ✕ (or pressing the Escape key twice) immediately replaces this page in your browser tab with Google so hitting the Back button will not reopen HelpHub. To remove it from your full history log, press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows) or Cmd + Y (Mac).

What happens when I text 988 or 741741?

Within moments, a trained crisis counselor replies via text. You can type at your own pace, share only what you feel comfortable sharing, and end the conversation whenever you wish.
